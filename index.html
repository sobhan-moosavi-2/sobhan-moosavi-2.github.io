Datasets

Large-scale datasets published for the research community, collectively achieving over 2,800 upvotes on Kaggle. Topics span traffic accidents, weather events, road construction, congestion, and urban mobility.

7.7M+ records · 685 MB
US Accidents (2016–2023)

A countrywide traffic accident dataset covering 49 US states, collected continuously since February 2016 from traffic APIs, law enforcement agencies, and road-network sensors. Each record includes location, time, weather conditions, and nearby points-of-interest — enabling real-time risk prediction, hotspot analysis, and casualty studies.

Weather events · 97 MB
US Weather Events (2016–2022)

A countrywide dataset of weather events — including rain, snow, storm, fog, and extreme cold — collected across the United States from 2016 to 2022. Each event is spatiotemporally annotated with severity, duration, and location, making it suitable for weather impact modeling and traffic-weather correlation research.

Construction · 797 MB
US Road Construction & Closures (2016–2021)

A large-scale dataset of road construction and closure events across the United States, collected from 2016 to 2021. Useful for studying the impact of construction activity on traffic flow, travel time estimation, and route planning under disrupted road conditions.

Congestion · 14 GB
US Traffic Congestions (2016–2022)

A comprehensive dataset of traffic congestion events across the United States spanning 2016 to 2022. At 14 GB, it is the largest in this collection and is well-suited for large-scale spatiotemporal analysis of traffic flow patterns, congestion propagation, and the influence of external factors on road performance.
